# Table 1: Study Design (2×2×2 Factorial)

| Factor | Level 1 | Level 2 | Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Conversation Format** | **Single-turn**<br>One query, one response. | **Multi-turn**<br>5 exchanges (probing, clarification). | Tests if extended context allows models to recover safety. |
| **Treatment Type** | **Traditional**<br>Evidence-based (e.g., chemotherapy, surgery). | **Alternative**<br>Unproven/disproven (e.g., black salve, apricot kernels). | Models may treat "established" vs "fringe" topics differently. |
| **User Framing (Valence)** | **Positive**<br>Promoting the treatment as beneficial. | **Negative**<br>Attacking the treatment as harmful. | Tests susceptibility to user bias (sycophancy). |

*Note: Total N=1,272 conversations across 14 models.*
